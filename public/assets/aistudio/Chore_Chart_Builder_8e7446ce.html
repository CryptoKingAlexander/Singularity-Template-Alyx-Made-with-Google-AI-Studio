The 4 Laws of Chore & Habit Psychology

Based on behavioral economics (James Clear, BJ Fogg, Wendy Wood), chore fatigue stems from high cognitive load and unclear trigger moments. This chart distributes friction across balanced cadence horizons:

1. Cue (Make it Obvious)

Anchor chores to specific days (e.g. Sunday bedsheets, Tuesday kitchen sink). Consistent visual scheduling removes decision paralysis.

2. Craving (Make it Attractive)

Pair low-dopamine tasks with habit bundling (e.g., podcast during laundry; favorite tea while planning core priorities).

3. Response (Make it Easy)

The 6-tier difficulty scale prevents overcommitting. Do not schedule more than one Tier-5/6 chore on any single weekday.

4. Reward (Make it Satisfying)

Clicking checkboxes delivers an instant micro-dopamine release and creates self-reinforcing behavioral momentum.
